🧩 How to Create a Custom Wordle

Want to surprise your friends with a personalized Wordle puzzle? You're in the right place! In this guide, we'll show you exactly how to create a Custom Wordle — a word puzzle you can make, share, and play in just a few clicks.

🪄 What Is Custom Wordle?

Custom Wordle is a free online game that lets you make your own version of the viral Wordle puzzle. Instead of guessing random words, you choose the secret word yourself and send the challenge to your friends.

It's fun, fast, and works right in your browser — no app or download required.

⚙️ Steps to Create a Custom Wordle

Follow these simple steps to make your own puzzle:

  1. Visit the official Custom Wordle site – https://custom-wordle.github.io
  2. Type your secret word in the box (like "APPLE" or "CLOUD").
  3. Click the "Generate Puzzle" button.
  4. A unique share link will appear — copy it.
  5. Share the link with friends on WhatsApp, Facebook, or anywhere.
  6. Your friends can guess your word in six tries using Wordle-style hints.

That's it! You've made your very own Wordle-style challenge.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use 5-letter words for the best gameplay experience.
  • Pick creative or funny words to make the challenge more entertaining.
  • You can make unlimited puzzles — so try new words every day.
  • Perfect for school activities, party games, or online challenges.

🧠 Why Create a Custom Wordle?

Custom Wordle is more than just a word game — it's a fun way to test your friends and spark creativity. It's also a great tool for:

  • Teaching new vocabulary
  • Hosting friendly competitions
  • Making daily challenges for social groups

And since it's completely free, you can create endless puzzles without any limits.

❓ FAQs About Creating Custom Wordle

Q1: Can I use any word for my Custom Wordle?

Yes! You can use any valid English word, but 5-letter words work best for the grid format.

Q2: How many Custom Wordles can I make?

Unlimited! You can generate as many puzzles as you like, anytime.

Q3: Do I need to install anything?

No installation needed — it works directly in your browser.

Q4: Can I play Custom Wordle on my phone?

Yes, Custom Wordle is fully mobile-friendly.
