💡 What is Custom Wordle?

Custom Wordle is an interactive word puzzle creator where players can make their own Wordle challenges. Instead of solving random daily puzzles, you get to decide the secret word — and your friends must try to guess it using hints from the colored tiles.

Each guess gives you instant feedback:

  • 🟩 Green: Correct letter in the right spot
  • 🟨 Yellow: Correct letter, wrong spot
  • ⬜ Gray: Letter not in the word

It's a perfect mix of creativity and logic — a word game that keeps you thinking and laughing!

🧩 How to Play Custom Wordle

  1. Go to Custom Wordle website.
  2. Type in your secret word.
  3. Copy the shareable link generated for your puzzle.
  4. Send the link to a friend or post it online.
  5. Watch as they try to guess your word in six attempts!
💬 Tip: Choose a tricky word for a fun challenge — or an inside joke only your friends will understand!
